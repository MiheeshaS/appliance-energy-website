KEY TAKEAWAY

Choosing a Television Is More Than Just Size

The data shows that LCD (LED) dominates the available technologies, 65-inch televisions are the most frequent, and KOGAN offers the greatest model variety. Larger screens generally consume more power, while screen size alone does not determine star rating.

Considering screen technology, size, brand and energy performance together can help consumers make more informed television comparisons.
