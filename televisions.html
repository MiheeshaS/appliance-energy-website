LCD (LED) Dominates the Available Market

KEY FINDING

Power Consumption Varies Between Brands

Average power consumption varies across television brands. Among the brands with the highest average power consumption, SPARK ELECTRONICS records 233.45 W, followed by EMETE at 182.03 W and SAMSUNG ELECTRONICS at 155.04 W.

CONCLUSION

What Does the Data Show?

The Australian television data shows that LCD (LED) is the dominant available screen technology, 65-inch televisions are the most frequent screen size, and KOGAN has the greatest number of different models.

In terms of energy performance, LCD has the lowest median power consumption, while power use generally increases with screen size. However, screen size shows no clear relationship with star rating. Power consumption also varies across brands, highlighting the importance of considering both television characteristics and energy performance when comparing available options.
