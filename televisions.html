WHAT THE DATA SHOWS

Key Takeaways for TV Buyers

LCD (LED) dominates the available screen technologies, 65-inch TVs are the most frequent size, and KOGAN offers the greatest model variety. LCD has the lowest median power consumption, while larger screen sizes generally consume more power.

Screen size does not show a clear relationship with star rating, and power consumption also varies between brands. Considering both television features and energy performance can therefore support more informed comparisons.
